---
title: "Bitter root"
date: 2026-11-20
summary: "what evil of night; blind, without sight, yet a plank in my own eye"
tags: [soften, sickness, twisted, submission]
---

Songs of sickness
Sweeter than honey
Viler than vomit

Enticing, self-righteousness
Bitterness of old

Suffocating in weeds,
Trapped, longing to be free...

Set your eyes on the Hope to be.

Flashes of lightning
Terrors of the mark
A smile
As one crushes their own heart

Why do we strike?
When we are cut,
Someone has to share,
For how could they dare!

"What evil of night
That _they_ cannot admit their plight"
Indeed
What evil of night
That _they_ cannot see their plight.

Twisting, gnarled
Slow erosion of the child
From chipper and glee
To poison sipped from the wound

Infested, infested
I am! 
What seaweed of sheol!
I am drowning Father,
Be not far...

Slow cooked and slightly charred,
Steaming a little,
I come close
Warming from the chill

"
Eat and be satisfied
For this is My body
For from the Father's choice
His will is done, so obey and rejoice!

And this is My blood
Now, My child,
For all your sin against Me
I will call you My own
"

We do not deserve yet
We cannot question if it was enough
It is not the point anymore
And to do so would be to spit on His cost

Soften toward and soften within
Draw near to His house
For no matter the sin
To us andor to Him
To Him only is the account
And so assuredly,
Enter in...
