The dark encompasses
The dead surround
Still
and cold
The freshness of winter
O to feel alive
On starving
And driven horse plough
On sleepless
And broken world around

Thank you Jesus
What overwhelming Grace

And when must it come nigh
The pit for which I give all to lie
At rest in cold and mourn
In bitterness of death
The end of the trudge
And rid of life's complex worthwhile edges
Drawing cuts deep
And twisting sight into voided smudges
The ink richly seeps
Poison of his lies
Drawing life from every seam

Yet
What God has made, none can break
What God has given, _none_ can take...

Oh what a Love
What perfect sacrifice
What uncomprehensible gift
What beautiful light

For the world is as night
But You run deeper
My love and my Life

So I'll thank you in the day
And thank you in the night
Thank you in clear sight
Thank you in twisting blind

You are everything to me
You are _beyond_ pure light
