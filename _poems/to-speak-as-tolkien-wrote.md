To speak as Tolkien wrote
Let honey in the throat
Warm as sweet melody on higher note

Indeed the seed from Tolkien speak
Has passed in many a furrow
Yet in which of these
Has its day truly been?

For when is word truly heard
Entrenched without time
or hidden in a rugged dime

Ere this day
I have erelong been at bay
For until speech of mine beseech
My mind had been mapped quietly dour
Trapped in the till of this hour

Yet now, I may ask
of Life
At last!
For is mind made for thus play
or perhaps made for each its day

March forward speech of old
vanquish the tillers of the tower
lead on, take back!
dust of gardens grown till sour...
sleak and slippery yet
roots now ripped of tact.

So over hill and under hill
hurling rocks and cracking caves
a secret place
my mind now craves

yet not truly
for whenst come nigh
it would be a little more than dry
for nothing outside longing and dream
and nothing under full lock and key
can be what truly promised be

nothing
that is

bar One.

so fire and wind and stolen toes
in mind I sleep knowing its close
dream on my son till break of first hour
in the land that reminds us
_of Him_, perfect, 
and of His to which we started:
_free_ from sour.

---

It is not speech of Tolkien which thrusts its want
but speech of old that brings mind to freely gaze

outside the mire, casting off tire and taunt
to remember this is not all and—
suddenly I find—
to this my heart again ablaze.

So perhaps even write as Tolkien spoke
but mind not his nor ours
to be what not man has...
is to wile away these precious, precious...

**To speak as Tolkien wrote**
_Ben Wagstaff - 00:30am_
