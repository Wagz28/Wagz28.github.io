---
title: "Serpent error"
date: 2026-11-23
summary: "Little one, that is oil not water!"
tags: [wonder, joy, riddle-like, free]
---

&emsp;&emsp;Joy, sing joy

Spun and young
his timeless practice
to wrung what has soaked
to chase death and bring blood of life

&emsp;&emsp;For natural love and endurance have departed me

Webs upon webs
Vile upon gleem
Maggots in swagger
Yet who holds the dagger

So death, let it come!
Where art thou sting
Only treasure you seem to bring

A Father laughs
A Child chuckles
Use water not oil, little one
For you may be caught in the flame
Of your own gnawing insane

So _rage_, yes _rage_
let it burn
try, please try
for you only Glorify

&emsp;&emsp;Let darkness come

When does light shine brightest?
When is food most loved?
And gifts most unrepayable?

Take me there little serpent
Drive me to the edge and taunt
Need I remind you of His heel
Or the dust that you eat

Come, come, little one
see the Father working
join in dance
in your own desire's consuming fire

break such bonds, cast off such chords
breatheeeee violence
rage, yes rage,
and stand in awe... amazed

&emsp;&emsp;For darkness is only lack of light

For what is it you have?
_Knowledge_?
_To do what with?!_
Have you not observed creation?

Have you seen the seed that grows into a
Time which winter comes and goes, a
Sign bright in the sky — yet only one?

&emsp;&emsp;And the things above cannot be below

Fool!
Read His words
Watch His step
Take note as you look
To the dagger placed in your own heart

For as the stars threw down their spears
Only the shaken were terrified
Only those in death,
consumed.

&emsp;&emsp;And what part have we in such things?

Sweet, sweet fellowship
Dancing, singing
Riding far and resting deep

Smoothed and sharpened
Crisp, unshaken
Swimming, sinking
splashing droplets of joy

Slicing, dicing,
A feast for the new family
Greens and seeds
For what else could there be?

&emsp;&emsp;For in our joy we have already recieved with thanksgiving

Indeed recieved.
