---
title: "Waterfall"
date: 2026-10-16
summary: "roaring, soaring, the greatness of my Lord"
tags: [glorify, greatness, death, riddle-like, christian]
---

As the roaring of waterfalls
Deep within the fold
In valleys untrodden
In the cruelest of colds

For what is this glass and fire
Except to burn with lyre
Reaching within to vastness
Twisted and haunted with calluses

Manufactured and sweet
Poison yet such a treat
One bite for a little more
But line upon line is what we are called for.

Rumbling and mighty
Hour after hour
Deep, deep... and unseen
What could we possibly have to do with it

Far, far
Its river flows
Winding, comprising
Even that that was foe

Drink, drink
Of life in its time
Weep, weep
For the beauty of its Name

And on a distant hill
My making was made still
For on it I died
A fight I thankfully did not survive

And the sight which held the dagger
Was a man ripped up and ragged
And so in fine clothes I stood
Realising my poverty, 
rotting and dead
My death for which he bled

Like marching legions
And sweet sweet fellowship
True life not living
But breathing it all in

So I follow Your line
Dancing, dancing
Spinning and grinning
In the river from your side

Tears carry weight
The tiniest sheds much
For when we are filled
We tend to overspill

Listen to its sound
Listen and be bound
Bound in Love, bound for Peace
For none of it,
Depends on you.

You are dead.
So join me in the water
For nothing else is left
And nothing could compare.
